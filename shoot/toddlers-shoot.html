Toddlers Photoshoot in Dharmapuri

Toddlers rarely sit still, and that is exactly what makes their photos special. PV Delight Pix captures their curiosity, movement, smiles and tiny moods through patient, playful photography in studio or outdoor settings.

What makes our toddlers photoshoot special?

Play-led direction: We use games, prompts and movement instead of forcing stiff poses.

Milestone focus: The session can capture birthdays, first steps, favorite toys and growing personality.

Studio or outdoor: Choose a controlled setup or a more natural lifestyle shoot outside.

Parent-friendly pace: We keep enough time for breaks, snacks and mood changes.

What is included?

  • Solo toddler portraits with expressive close-ups.
  • Parent, sibling and family frames.
  • Indoor themed setups or outdoor lifestyle sessions.
  • Best suited for capturing the busy, funny and fast-changing toddler stage.
