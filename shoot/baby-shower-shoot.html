Baby Shower Photography in Dharmapuri

Home / Baby Shower

Celebrate your baby shower with warm, story‑driven photographs.

A baby shower is one of the sweetest milestones before your little one arrives. PV Delight Pix captures the blessings, décor, games and quiet moments between you and your family so that this day lives on long after the celebrations end.

Why choose PV Delight Pix for your baby shower in Dharmapuri?

Gentle, unobtrusive coverage: The team documents rituals, blessings and candid reactions without interrupting the flow of the function.

Focus on family bonds: Grandparents, siblings, friends and the parents‑to‑be are all photographed with care so everyone feels included.

Pastel‑friendly edits: Soft, flattering colours that work beautifully with baby shower décor, floral setups and ethnic wear.

Flexible locations: Shoots can be done at your decorated home, function hall or our studio in Dharmapuri.

Types of baby shower photography services we offer

  • Home ceremonies: Intimate events with close family, documented from preparations to blessings and cake cutting.
  • Hall & banquet sessions: Full coverage of stage events, decor, games and group photos with guests.
  • Couple & maternity portraits: Calm, beautifully lit portraits of the parents‑to‑be before or after the function.
  • Detail storytelling: Focus on décor elements such as invites, favors, centrepieces and gifts that you carefully planned.

What you can expect when you book PV Delight Pix

  • Pre‑event call to understand rituals, themes, colours and key family members to focus on.
  • Friendly guidance for group photos so everyone looks their best without long waiting times.
  • Professionally edited images delivered as soft copies, with options for prints and albums.
  • Packages that can combine baby shower coverage with future newborn or maternity sessions.
